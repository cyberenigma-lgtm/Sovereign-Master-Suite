## 🔬 Cross-Repository Architectural Bridge

The **Sovereign Master Suite** acts as a unified perimetric gate to compile, translate, and audit low-level assets across the 6 main public pillars of the ecosystem:

1. 🜏 [**NeuroCOBOL-V3.0**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/NeuroCOBOL-V3.0): Exact 18-digit BCD math (`COMP-3`), packing binaries into page-aligned structures and stamping the authority seal `0x4A4D4D43` (`*JMMC*`) at offset 8.
2. 🛡️ [**UNIVERSAL-POLYGLOT-LAYER-UPL**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/UNIVERSAL-POLYGLOT-LAYER-UPL): Polyglot Intermediate Representation (`UPL-IR`) executing cross-language flows (C, Python, Rust, Go) at L1 cache speeds.
3. ⌨️ [**MultiLang-ASM**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/MultiLang-ASM): Universal x86_64 translation supporting 56 languages and regional dialects with reversible architecture.
4. 🚀 [**NeuroUniversalASM**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/NeuroUniversalASM): Direct-to-silicon compilation for 51 languages natively (compiles directly into raw opcodes, no intermediate English translation).
5. 🧠 [**NeuroWill-Code**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/NeuroWill-Code): C++ Neural-to-Binary engine in charge of parsing semantic intent declarations into executable instruction packets.
6. 🔍 [**Neuro-probe**](https://github.com/cyberenigma-lgtm/Neuro-probe): Parallel multi-emulator validator that spins kernel binaries simultaneously across QEMU, VirtualBox, and Bochs to map hardware divergence in seconds.
